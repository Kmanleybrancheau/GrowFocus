MVP Scope & Feature Backlog
The Ultimate Product Vision

GrowFocus is a free, browser-based study app that keeps students on task by locking their attention on their study material and adding time whenever they get distracted. It is built for students who struggle to stay focused when studying alone.

The Launch Goal (v1.0 MVP)

To launch a functional version by November 27th 2026, focusing strictly on core value: a focused, distraction-aware study session built around the student's own material.

Feature Prioritization Matrix
Must Have (MVP Lineup)
Feature	Description	Why it is critical for launch

- Tab Checking	While a session is running, GrowFocus detects when the student leaves the app (switches tabs, minimizes, or exits fullscreen).	This is the core of the product. Without distraction detection, GrowFocus is just a timer.
- Study-Specific Material	The student pastes or selects a section of material they want to study, and the session is built around it.	Gives students a reason to stay in the app, since their study content lives there.
- Adaptive Study Times	The student sets a session length. Every time a distraction is detected, extra time is automatically added to the session.	This is the "consequence" that makes the focus lock meaningful. It builds directly on Tab Checking.

Nice to Have (Post-Launch Backlog)
Feature	Description	Why it can wait
- Camera Access	Uses the camera to detect phone use or looking away from the material for too long.	Technically complex (computer vision), raises privacy concerns, and needs camera permissions. The core loop works without it.
- Post-Study Statistics	A short quiz after each session that tracks improvement over time.	Requires quiz generation and progress storage. It adds value, but students can still get a focused session without it.
