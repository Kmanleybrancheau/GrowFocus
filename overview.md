Tab checking
- while using GrowFocus it will check to see if the application is currently the only thing on your screen.
Study specific information
- GrowFocus will have an option where you can provide a section of material you would like to study allowing you to study what you need most.
Camera access
- GrowFocus accesses the camera to ensure you are not distracted on your phone or looking away from the study material too long.
Adaptive study times
- you can choose how long you plan to study for and if you open another tab or use your phone GrowFocus will automatically add more time to your study session.
Post study statistics
- After each study session GrowFocus will provide a small quiz to track how much you've improved since starting to use GrowFocus
